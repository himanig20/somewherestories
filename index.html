A little about us

Hi, we’re Himani and Medha.

We’re sisters who love travel, and we got into making content because we kept noticing the same things: the morning light, a good breakfast table, the corner of a room that makes you want to stay longer. Somewhere Stories is where we turn that into short videos and photos.

Some trips it’s one of us, some trips it’s both, and every stay gets the same attention. We’re just getting started, so we keep things simple and do them well: one reel, a set of photos, delivered on time.
