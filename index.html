A little about us

Curious about places. Careful with stories.

Somewhere Stories is a creator-led travel and hospitality content studio based in India. We’re drawn to places with character — the welcoming corners, the morning light, the details that make a stay feel personal.

Led by Himani, we’re building a collection of thoughtful visual work for boutique stays, homestays, cafés and destinations. We’re at the beginning of the journey, and believe in being transparent, collaborative and intentional with every project.
